FIX TTS WINERROR 206 - BOOM REVIEW

DANH CHO MAY KHACH DANG CHAY EXE:
1. Giai nen ZIP vao mot thu muc co quyen ghi, vi du Desktop\FIX_TTS.
2. Dong BoomReview.
3. Chuot phai FIX_TTS_WIN206.cmd > Run as administrator.
4. Bam phim theo huong dan, sau do khoi dong lai Windows va thu lai.
5. Neu van loi: trong BoomReview chon thu muc xuat ngan C:\BR, dat ten phim ngan (Phim01) va tao lan xuat moi tu kich ban da xac nhan.
   Khong xoa thu muc cu; khong doi ten/di chuyen du an dang dang do.
   Lan xuat moi co the tao lai TTS va tinh phi. Neu da co voice thanh cong, giu lai de nguoi ho tro kiem tra truoc.

GIOI HAN:
Bat LongPathsEnabled chi hieu qua voi ung dung ho tro longPathAware.
Day la fix cau hinh Windows, KHONG phai ban BoomReview.exe moi.
Neu exe khong ho tro, dung duong dan xuat ngan hoac ban exe da build lai.

HOAN TAC:
File chay tu luu gia tri cu vao LongPathsEnabled_before.reg trong cung thu muc.
Nhap file .reg nay voi quyen admin neu muon phuc hoi; khoi dong lai Windows.

DANH CHO BAN CHAY MA NGUON / NGUOI BUILD:
Thu muc source\engine chua 2 file da sua. Sao luu file cu truoc khi thay the
vao ma nguon cung phien ban, roi khoi dong lai hoac build lai ung dung.
Khong chep cac file .py nay vao thu muc exe Nuitka de mong ghi de ma da bien dich.
Da chay 8 kiem thu offline, bao gom duong dan xuat dai va tiep tuc job.
